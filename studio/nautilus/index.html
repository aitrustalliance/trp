Voyage No. 1

Nautilus

The world drowned, and one power built the last great machine mind. Its airship, the Leviathan, hunts every vessel that still sails free. Your Nautilus runs on open rules that anyone can read, and those rules are all that keep it alive.

How To Play

  • Tap a threat to fire a harpoon at it. Harpoons run on steam, so pick your shots.
  • Drag up or down to change depth and slip past depth charges. Deeper water strains the hull.
  • Watch the gauges. They read your vessel's evidence, and the open TRP engine checks it ten times a second. When a reading crosses a rule, the telegraph rings Half Ahead or All Stop on its own.
  • Stopping keeps you alive but costs leagues. Shoot down fog, decoys, firebrands, boarding drones and forged commands before they reach you.
  • The Writ is the Leviathan's real weapon. If it lands, your rules are overwritten for a while and nothing will stop you from sailing into harm. It takes three harpoons.

Keyboard: arrows for depth, space to fire at the nearest threat, E to see why the telegraph rang, P to pause. Tap the telegraph for the same explanation.
