Voyage No. 1

Nautilus

The world drowned, and one power built the last great machine mind. Its airship, the Leviathan, hunts every vessel that still sails free. Your Nautilus runs on open rules that anyone can read, and those rules are all that keep it alive.

New aboard? The shakedown cruise takes one minute and teaches the helm.

Standing orders

How To Play

  • Tap a threat to fire a harpoon at it. Harpoons run on steam, so pick your shots.
  • Drag up or down to change depth and slip past depth charges. Deeper water strains the hull.
  • Watch the gauges. They read your vessel's evidence, and the open rules check it ten times a second. When a reading crosses a limit, the telegraph rings Half Ahead or All Stop on its own.
  • Stopping keeps you alive and costs leagues. Shoot down fog, decoys, firebrands, boarding drones and forged commands before they reach you.
  • The Decree is the Leviathan's real weapon. If it lands, your rules are overwritten for a while and nothing will stop you from sailing into harm. It takes three harpoons.
  • The All Stop lever beside the telegraph is yours. Pull it to hold the ship still, for instance beside a giant clam, which opens only for a ship at rest. Stopping costs leagues and is always your call.
  • Salvage sinks from every threat you destroy or survive. Collect it, and the page goes to the library in the salon. Sea chests on the seabed and pearls in the clams carry the deeper pages.

Keyboard: arrows for depth, space to fire at the nearest threat, X for the All Stop lever, E to see why the telegraph rang, P to pause. Tap the telegraph for the same explanation.
