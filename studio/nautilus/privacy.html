Privacy Note

Nautilus is a free game that runs in your browser. You can play it without giving us anything.

What Stays On Your Device

Your scores, brass, leagues, unlocks and settings are saved in your browser's local storage on this device. We never see them unless you join the crew. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Joining The Crew

Joining is optional. If you join, you send a captain name, an email address, what brings you aboard, and your game progress (leagues, brass, best score and streak). We use this to send Nautilus and Trust Requirements Profile (TRP) updates, to honor your progress in future seasons, and to reach out if you said you want to contribute or that your organization needs trust rules.

The form is delivered by Web3Forms, a service that turns form submissions into email. Web3Forms processes your submission to deliver it. The information is then held by the AI Trust Alliance and the organizations that help run it and its programs, and only for the purposes above.

We do not sell your information. Brass, medals and unlocks are game rewards with no cash value.

Leaving

Every email has a way to stop further emails. To have your crew record deleted, write to the address listed in the repository.

Other Services

The game makes no other network requests while you play. Links to GitHub, X and other sites take you to those sites, which have their own policies.
